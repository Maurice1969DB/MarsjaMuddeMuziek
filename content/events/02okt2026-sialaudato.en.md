### Sia laudato, San Francesco! The Canticle of the Sun
Anime Beatitudinis Cantando performs the animated concert program [Sia laudato, San Francesco](/en/programmas/sialaudato), in which ancient musical gems, vivid visuals and a joyful spirit together tell the story of Francis of Assisi. The program celebrates the 800th anniversary year of Francis and his famous Canticle of the Sun.

#### Date and time:
Friday 2 October 2026, 8:00 PM.

#### Address:
Hillegondakerk, Rotterdam.
