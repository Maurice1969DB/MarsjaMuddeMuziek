### Music on Sunday at De Oude Lambertus
Marsja Mudde performs medieval and Renaissance music during the *Muziek op Zondag* (Music on Sunday) service at De Oude Lambertus in Engelen. Coffee is served afterwards. More information is available on the [church's website](https://www.kerk-engelen.nl/kalender/action~oneday/exact_date~1-11-2026/).

#### Date and time:
Sunday 1 November 2026, 10:30–11:30 AM.

#### Address:
De Oude Lambertus, De Kerkhof 3, 5221 AJ Engelen ('s-Hertogenbosch), the Netherlands.
