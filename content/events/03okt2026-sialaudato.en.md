### Sia laudato, San Francesco! The Canticle of the Sun
Anime Beatitudinis Cantando performs the animated concert program [Sia laudato, San Francesco](/en/programmas/sialaudato), in which ancient musical gems, vivid visuals and a joyful spirit together tell the story of Francis of Assisi. On 3 October, the day Francis died, his Canticle of the Sun sounds exactly 800 years after his death.

#### Date and time:
Saturday 3 October 2026, 8:00 PM.

#### Address:
Remonstrantse kerk, Alkmaar.
